ACADEMIA · UNILAG ARCHIVE
Chapter 01

Academic Foundations & Critical Scholarship

2014 – 2018 · University of Lagos

Archival Document: Contributing sub-editor on the landmark 145-page Presidential Committee on North-East Initiative (PCNI) Report under President Muhammadu Buhari's administration.

Alfred earned his Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in English and Literary Studies from the prestigious University of Lagos (UNILAG). His academic training immersed him in dramatic theory, semiotics, phonology, and post-colonial African literature.

During this period, he founded My WORLD of Literature (TWOL), designing the platform from scratch to publish penetrating theater reviews (such as his dramaturgy critique of Dr. Chris Anyokwu's The Far Country at the UNILAG Arts Theatre) and sociopolitical essays on Nigerian legislative debates.

He also served as a contributing sub-editor on national publications, including the landmark 145-page Presidential Committee on North-East Initiative (PCNI) Report under President Muhammadu Buhari's administration, honing an eye for high-stakes institutional precision.

PRIMARY ECONOMIC FIELDWORK
Chapter 02

The 551 SMEs Lagos Commercial Field Study

Primary Economic Field Research · 9 Districts

Between 2021 and 2023, Alfred spearheaded an intensive ground-level research expedition across 9 major commercial hubs in Lagos State—including Ikeja, Yaba, Lekki, Victoria Island, Oshodi, Alaba International, Balogun Market, Surulere, and Agege.

Through direct in-person interviews and operational audits with 551 small and medium business owners, he mapped real-world bottlenecks: cash-flow opacity, erratic inventory reconciliation, over-reliance on informal WhatsApp trade, and an absence of lightweight, accessible digital tools.

“Software built for emerging markets cannot afford decorative latency. When bandwidth fluctuates and currency devalues daily, clarity is not an aesthetic choice—it is survival.”

— Alfred E. Williams · Field Notes on 551 Lagos Enterprises

This rigorous empirical field study became the foundation for his software philosophy: building intuitive, zero-bloat web tools designed for maximum resilience in bandwidth-constrained, volatile macroeconomic environments.
