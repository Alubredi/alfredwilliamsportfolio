01 / The Mandate

Building Trust with Discerning Parents

For forward-thinking parents in West Africa seeking world-class international schooling for their children, selecting an academic institution is a profound, high-stakes decision. Parents demand total transparency: curriculum rigor, international accreditation pathways (Cambridge IGCSE, SAT, TOEFL), and evidence of scholar growth.

GoFar International Academy required a modern digital portal that communicated high academic gravitas while demystifying the admission process for parents navigating from mobile devices.

02 / Technical Architecture

Friction-Free Ingestion & Mobile Accessibility

As Lead Web Architect, I structured the digital portal to guide parents naturally from curiosity to confirmed campus tour bookings:

  • [01] Segmented Curricular Navigation: Created dedicated discovery tracks for Early Years Foundation, Primary Excellence, Secondary College, and International Exam Prep.
  • [02] Instant WhatsApp Ingestion: Embedded context-aware chat buttons linking prospective parents directly to the admissions director with pre-filled grade-level inquiries.
  • [03] Zero-Bloat Performance: Built with semantic HTML5 and vanilla CSS, achieving instant render times even during peak school registration seasons.
03 / Community Stewardship

Humanizing the Academy on Social Media

To complement the institutional portal, I managed day-to-day visual storytelling across GoFar's official social channels (@gofar_international).

I replaced sterile corporate flyers with vibrant, human-centered photojournalism: classroom science experiments, robotics presentations, student sports victories, and teacher philosophy spotlights, cultivating a community of engaged parents.
