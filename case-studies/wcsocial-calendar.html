01 / The Mandate

Bringing Order to Multi-Channel Social Operations

Publishing across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and X without a centralized masterplan leads to message fragmentation, missed campaign deadlines, and brand fatigue.

I engineered the WC Social Media Calendar as an operational command center: establishing precise monthly objectives, primary audience KPIs, an educational-first selling philosophy, and a disciplined 3x weekly cadence designed for maximum authority.

02 / System Architecture

Editorial Grid & Frictionless Scheduling

  • [01] Luxury Editorial Aesthetic: Designed with high-contrast serif typography, gold hairline borders, and dark backdrops to elevate strategic seriousness.
  • [02] Multi-Channel Synchronization: Synchronizes copy, visual assets, and hashtags across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and X.
  • [03] Goal-to-Metric Mapping: Integrates quantitative KPIs directly into weekly planning blocks, ensuring every post serves a strategic business outcome.
