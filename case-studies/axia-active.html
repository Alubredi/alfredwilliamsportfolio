01 / The Mandate

Elevating Fitness Athleisure into a Lifestyle Culture

Emerging fitness apparel brands face intense global competition from legacy sportswear conglomerates. To command premium pricing and foster authentic community loyalty, AXiA Active needed more than a basic storefront; they required a digital presence that radiated athletic discipline, lifestyle elegance, and effortless purchasing velocity.

Alfred took over the complete digital footprint: engineering the responsive web storefront, eliminating friction in the sizing and checkout process, and building performance ad campaigns that convert casual Instagram scrollers into repeat brand advocates.

02 / UX & Storefront Engineering

Mobile-First Checkout Mechanics

Over 82% of athleisure traffic originates on mobile devices through Instagram and TikTok feeds. Any friction—slow image decoding, clunky drop-downs, or multi-step checkout redirects—results in catastrophic cart abandonment.

  • [01] Instant Slide-Out Bag: Enabled instant product variation selection and drawer checkout without forcing a page reload, preserving user momentum.
  • [02] High-Fidelity Lookbooks: Implemented responsive progressive image loading for high-resolution studio shots and athletic lifestyle photography.
  • [03] Multi-Payment Gateway Integration: Integrated secure local and international card checkout channels, enabling frictionless customer purchases.
03 / Performance Marketing

Full-Funnel Meta Ads & Community Stewardship

Beyond web development, Alfred architected and managed the direct customer acquisition pipeline:

Top of Funnel (Prospecting): Curated high-energy gym workout videos and activewear product reels targeting fitness enthusiasts and urban professionals across major metropolitan centers.

Bottom of Funnel (Retargeting): Set up robust Meta Conversions API (CAPI) tracking to serve tailored dynamic catalog ads to users who initiated checkout, recovering lost sales and maximizing Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).
