01 / The Challenge

Signal in a Sea of Economic Noise

In developing African markets, corporate decision-makers, hedge fund analysts, and diaspora founders face an uphill battle: financial data is heavily fragmented across non-standardized government gazettes, informal black-market currency reports, and sensationalized headlines.

Renalytica was founded to cut through this clutter. The mandate was uncompromising: create a lightning-fast, high-trust digital clearinghouse that tracks volatile FX shifts (such as the Central Bank of Nigeria's NAFEM rate), sovereign debt milestones, and deep commercial dossiers—delivering institutional clarity without corporate bloat.

02 / Technical Architecture

Proprietary Web Engineering with Zero Bloat

As the Lead Full-Stack Web Developer and Systems Architect, Alfred built the entire Renalytica web application from the initial blueprint to live cloud deployment. Rejecting heavy, dependency-riddled front-end frameworks that degrade bandwidth in emerging markets, he engineered a bespoke, pure-standard architecture:

  • [01] Sub-100ms Page Load Speeds: Tailored CSS variables, zero-dependency layout mechanics, and progressive image optimization delivering instant readability even on 3G cellular connections.
  • [02] Live Financial & Currency Ticker: Built an automated real-time rates strip tracking the official USD/NGN exchange rate, Brent crude oil benchmarks, and monetary policy rate adjustments.
  • [03] Scalable Research Archive: Architected a searchable, tag-indexed repository powering over 2,500 macroeconomic dossiers, industry whitepapers, and corporate financial analyses.
