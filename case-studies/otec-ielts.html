01 / The Mandate

Eliminating Subjectivity in IELTS Speaking Prep

For African candidates seeking global study and migration opportunities, the IELTS Speaking exam is frequently the most anxious hurdle. Preparation is traditionally subjective: tutors give vague verbal feedback ("you sounded good") without quantifying where the student lost points.

OTEC Nigeria commissioned Alfred to build an objective diagnostic software tool that examiners, tutors, and self-studying scholars can use to systematically score performance across the official British Council / IDP IELTS assessment criteria.

02 / Rubric Engineering

Real-Time Band Computation & Multi-Part Evaluation

The tool was engineered to reflect official examination standards:

  • [01] 4 Core Assessment Pillars: Segmented metrics for Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy, and Pronunciation.
  • [02] Weighted Sub-Skill Sliders: Granular diagnostic evaluation covering Pacing & Flow, Sentence Linking, Discourse Markers, Word Stress, Sentence Stress, Weak Forms, and Enunciation.
  • [03] Instant Band Averaging: Reactive JavaScript mathematical calculation continuously updating the live overall band badge from Band 1.0 to Band 9.0 in real time.
