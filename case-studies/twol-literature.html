01 / The Genesis

A Modern Digital Agora for African Letters

Founded during Alfred's academic tenure at the University of Lagos (UNILAG), My WORLD of Literature was established with a singular conviction: rigorous literary theory and cultural criticism should not remain sequestered behind lecture hall podiums.

Alfred designed the publication himself, curating serif typography hierarchies, comfortable line measures, and clear categorical taxonomies. He used the platform to publish penetrating reviews of contemporary Nigerian drama, dissect the sociopolitics of Afrobeats, and challenge regressive legislative decisions.
