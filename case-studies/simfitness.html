01 / The Mandate

Eliminating Friction in Personal Fitness Planning

Most fitness applications suffer from overwhelming user onboarding and cluttered interfaces. Users are inundated with ads, complicated calorie counting spreadsheets, and rigid schedules that lead to quick drop-offs.

SimFitness was conceived to solve this problem through elegant simplicity: an all-in-one, highly visual web application that delivers personalized workout plans, smart nutrition recommendations, real-time BMI metrics, and fitness education in an intuitive, distraction-free environment.

02 / Technical Architecture

Reactive Calculation Engines & Glassmorphic Design

Alfred built SimFitness with an emphasis on instant feedback and micro-interactions:

  • [01] Real-Time Dynamic Calculators: Integrated instantaneous body mass index (BMI), basal metabolic rate (BMR), and caloric requirement calculations that update dynamically as inputs adjust.
  • [02] Dark Glassmorphism Interface: Styled with deep radial gradient backdrops, frosted backdrop-filters, and vibrant micro-gradients to deliver an immersive aesthetic.
  • [03] Modular Routine Builder: Architected structured workout modules categorized by muscle groups, skill levels, and equipment availability.
