CLIENT FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Web Builds

Everything you need to know before initiating a project with Alfred.

Why don't you use WordPress or Elementor? −
WordPress and heavy visual page-builders load dozens of external stylesheets, JavaScript libraries, and database queries. In Nigeria, where visitors browse on mobile 3G/4G networks, that extra bloat adds 5 to 10 seconds of loading delay, costing you over half your potential clients. Hand-coded vanilla web standards load in under 100 milliseconds, cannot be hacked through vulnerable plugins, and never require monthly update fixes.
Do I own the code once the project is finished? +
Yes, 100%. Once final payment is made, the entire codebase is transferred to your personal or company GitHub account. You are never held hostage or locked into paying monthly platform subscriptions.
Can I accept payments in Naira and foreign currencies? +
Yes. I integrate Paystack (for instant Naira cards, bank transfers, USSD) and Stripe (for international cards in USD, GBP, and EUR), complete with automated receipts and WhatsApp notifications.
What if I need updates or changes after launch? +
Every build comes with 14 to 30 days of complimentary post-launch support. Afterward, you can either manage edits yourself using simple documentation or retain me for ongoing monthly maintenance and digital marketing.
