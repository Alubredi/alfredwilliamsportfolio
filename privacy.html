Effective Date: October 20, 2026 · Standard Version 2.4. Operating in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA / NDPR) and international privacy frameworks (GDPR).

Stewardship Summary

I believe that digital privacy is a fundamental human right, not an optional preference. This portfolio does not track your personal identity, does not sell your contact details to data brokers, and does not install tracking pixels. When you transmit an inquiry via the Commission Terminal or correspond via email, your information is used solely to evaluate and execute your project commission.

1. Collection of Information

This web domain (alfredwilliams.dev / alfredwilliams.netlify.app) collects only minimal, necessary information through the following touchpoints:

  • Direct Commission Inquiries: When submitting details through the Commission Terminal or direct mail, you may provide your full name, corporate organization, email address, phone number, and project brief.
  • Aggregate Technical Telemetry: Standard, privacy-preserving server logs (IP address, browser type, referral URLs, time of visit) collected purely to monitor network integrity, security firewalls, and page load velocity.
  • Client Operational Assets: If you engage my professional services for web development, whitepaper drafting, or admissions coaching, confidential assets (transcripts, business models, financial data) are collected strictly under non-disclosure obligations.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data exclusively under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Execution: To communicate regarding prospective or active project contracts, scoping requirements, invoices, and deliverable handoffs.
  • Legitimate Interests: To protect our platform against malicious injection attacks, rate-limiting violations, and unauthorized intellectual property scraping.
  • Explicit Consent: Where you voluntarily contact me requesting admissions advisory, strategic consulting, or social system audits.

3. Data Retention and Confidentiality

Client briefs, proprietary research materials, and correspondence are retained only as long as necessary to satisfy the commercial engagement, maintain accounting records, or fulfill contractual warranties. We maintain strict local encryption and do not store sensitive client passwords or credentials on unsecured public clouds.

"Your confidential corporate briefings and personal academic statements remain sacred. They are never repurposed, anonymized for marketing, or exposed to third parties without your written consent."

4. Cookies & Local Storage

This website uses local browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) exclusively to store your interface preferences:

  • alfred_portfolio_theme: Remembers your selected theme (Light Mode or Dark Mode).
  • alfred_preloaded: Prevents the studio boot preloader animation from displaying repeatedly during the same session.

No third-party tracking cookies or behavioral advertising cookies are deposited on your device.

5. Your Statutory Rights

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, you enjoy full sovereignty over your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request a complete copy of all communication records and personal data we hold about you.
  • Right of Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or outdated contact information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request immediate permanent deletion of your inquiry data and historical communications.
