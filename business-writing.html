CLIENT FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Business Writing

Answers to common questions about timelines, design formats, and input materials.

Do I need to write anything before we begin? −
No extensive drafting is required from your side. We begin with a short 30-minute discovery call or a simple questionnaire where you share your company's core services, past projects, target audience, and any existing materials you have. I handle the research, outline, copy drafting, and visual design.
Are both the written text and the graphic design included? +
Yes, absolutely. Unlike agencies that make you hire a copywriter and a separate graphic designer, my packages deliver complete, finished, publication-ready documents. The text is crafted to fit the visual layout seamlessly.
What file formats do I receive? +
For Company Profiles, you receive a high-resolution 300DPI print-ready PDF and a lightweight compressed PDF for emailing and WhatsApp sharing. For Pitch Decks, you receive both an editable presentation file (PowerPoint / Figma) and a polished presentation PDF.
What if my team needs revisions or adjustments? +
Every package includes two comprehensive rounds of revisions. You can review the draft with your team, share feedback, and I refine both the text and visual styling until you are completely satisfied.
