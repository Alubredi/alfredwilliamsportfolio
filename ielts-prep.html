FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know.

Is this for IELTS Academic or IELTS General Training? −
We cater to both! If you are applying for postgraduate studies, medical licensing (PLAB/GMC, USMLE), or nursing registration, we train you on IELTS Academic. If you are applying for Canadian Express Entry, UK Skilled Worker, or Australian PR, we train you on IELTS General Training.
I have taken the IELTS before and scored 6.5. Can you help me reach 7.5 or 8.0? +
This is our core specialty. Most repeat candidates stay stuck at 6.5 because they repeat the same structural mistakes in Writing and pause too frequently in Speaking. During the first diagnostic session, we identify exactly what is holding you back and apply targeted interventions to gain that crucial 1.0 to 1.5 band jump within 4 weeks.
How are the live mocks conducted? +
Speaking mocks and reviews are conducted live 1-on-1 via Google Meet or Zoom. Alfred simulates an official IELTS examiner, recording your session and stopping to provide immediate corrections on pronunciation, grammatical complexity, and vocabulary choice.
How do I pay tuition? +
Tuition can be paid in Nigerian Naira (₦) via bank transfer, or in USD ($) / GBP (£) via card, PayPal, or international wire for candidates living in the UK, US, Canada, or Europe.
