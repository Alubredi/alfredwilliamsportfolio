Do my staff members need any coding or programming knowledge? +
Absolutely not. 100% of the training is built around natural language prompting and user-friendly software interfaces. If your staff can type an email or use Microsoft Word and WhatsApp, they will be completely comfortable in this training.
Do you deliver training on-site or virtually? +
Both. We deliver high-energy on-site workshops across Nigeria (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan) directly at your corporate headquarters or conference facility. For international clients or distributed teams, we deliver interactive virtual sessions via Zoom or Microsoft Teams with screen-sharing, breakout exercises, and live feedback.
Will our company confidential data or customer information be leaked? +
Data privacy is a foundational pillar of Module 04. We audit your team’s account settings to ensure history training toggles are disabled, show your team how to redact sensitive PII, and configure team workspace permissions so your proprietary contracts and intellectual property remain 100% confidential.
What AI tools will our team learn during the sessions? +
We focus on the tools that deliver the highest ROI in real-world business today: OpenAI ChatGPT (Advanced Data Analysis & Custom GPTs), Anthropic Claude (long-form document synthesis and proposal writing), Google Gemini (deep research and document intelligence), and specialized automation extensions. We also teach teams how to maximize free tools before recommending paid licenses.
What payment methods and invoicing do you accept? +
We provide formal corporate invoices and receipts. Payment is accepted in Nigerian Naira (₦) via direct corporate bank transfer, and in USD / GBP / EUR ($) via international wire transfer or card for overseas organizations. Standard commercial terms are 50% mobilization upfront and 50% upon workshop completion.
