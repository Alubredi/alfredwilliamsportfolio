Heavy site builders (like bloated WordPress theme bundles or unoptimized builder exports) introduce massive JavaScript payloads, slow TTFB, and recurring security vulnerabilities. Handcrafted standard HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript deliver sub-100ms render speeds, 100% design freedom, clean ownership, and zero monthly plugin subscriptions.

That is my defining superpower. You don't need to hire a developer who doesn't understand your business messaging, nor a copywriter who doesn't understand technical UX. Standard web and branding projects follow a structured 50-30-20 or 50-50 milestone framework: 50% mobilization deposit upon scope signing, 30% upon staging architecture approval, and 20% upon final production deployment and DNS/source handover.
