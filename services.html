Do you work with international clients outside Nigeria? +
Yes. I collaborate regularly with founders, consultants, and students across the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, and Europe. All engagements are managed asynchronously via GitHub, Notion, and scheduled Google Meet / Zoom checkpoints aligned to your timezone.
Why pure standard web code over heavy generic builders? +
Heavy site builders (like bloated WordPress theme bundles or unoptimized builder exports) introduce massive JavaScript payloads, slow TTFB, and recurring security vulnerabilities. Handcrafted standard HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript deliver sub-100ms render speeds, 100% design freedom, clean ownership, and zero monthly plugin subscriptions.
Can you handle both web development and content writing? +
That is my defining superpower. You don't need to hire a developer who doesn't understand your business messaging, nor a copywriter who doesn't understand technical UX. I provide end-to-end synergy: high-performance code and authoritative, conversion-focused prose under one single accountability partner.
What is your typical payment milestone schedule? +
Standard web and branding projects follow a structured 50-30-20 or 50-50 milestone framework: 50% mobilization deposit upon scope signing, 30% upon staging architecture approval, and 20% upon final production deployment and DNS/source handover. Local payments via Bank Transfer/Paystack; international via Stripe, Wise, or Direct Wire.
What post-launch support and training do you provide? +
Every commercial package includes 30 to 60 days of dedicated post-launch support. I provide recorded Loom walkthroughs, complete technical documentation, and hands-on CMS/content publishing tutorials for your team so you maintain full operational sovereignty.
